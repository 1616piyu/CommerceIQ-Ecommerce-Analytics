
# CommerceIQ – Business Requirements Document

## 1. Business Context
An e-commerce marketplace wants to understand its sales
performance, customer purchasing behavior, delivery
operations, seller performance and customer satisfaction.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Analyze sales and order trends.
- Identify high-value customer segments.
- Measure repeat purchase behavior.
- Evaluate delivery performance.
- Identify categories associated with low reviews.
- Compare seller performance.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

## 3. Stakeholders
- Executive Management
- Sales and Finance Teams
- Marketing Team
- Operations Team
- Customer Experience Team

## 4. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total Orders | Count of distinct orders |
| GMV | Sum of product item prices |
| Average Order Value | GMV / eligible orders |
| Freight Value | Sum of item freight charges |
| Repeat Customer Rate | Customers with 2+ orders / purchasing customers |
| Average Review Score | Mean review score |
| On-Time Delivery Rate | Delivered orders delivered by estimated date / eligible delivered orders |
| Cancellation Rate | Cancelled orders / total orders |

## 5. Scope
- Data profiling and cleaning
- Relational database design
- SQL analytics
- Python exploratory analysis
- Power BI reporting
- Predictive analytics

## 6. Expected Deliverables
- Validated dataset
- Data dictionary
- Relational database
- SQL analysis scripts
- Interactive dashboards
- Predictive models
- Business recommendations

## 7. Data Limitations
- Historical data covers 2016–2018.
- Dataset is anonymized.
- Actual marketing expenditure is unavailable.
- Actual operating costs and net profit are unavailable.
- Predictions must be evaluated before business use.
